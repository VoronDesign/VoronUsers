# V0.2 Door with Foam Tape (for extending travel)

I installed a Dragon Burner toolhead onto my Voron 0.2r1, and ~2mm of Y travel is lost. However I am mean and want the travel back.

Then I spotted the foam tape from my Voron Trident's BOM, so I decided to give it a change to save my V0.2 Y travel in a cheap (zero cost for me) way.

## BOM

All parts for the original tophat/door assembly are not counted.

- 2m of Foam Tape (5mm wide/3mm thick) (see sourcing guides of V2.4/VT)
- 6x M3x10mm BHCS (replacing the M3x8mm ones for the front panel of the top hat)

## Assembly

- Remove built-in support from the two hinge parts.
- Apply foam tape to the top/left/right sides of the front tophat panel and the bottom/left/right sides of the door panel. (Don't apply tape on the bottom side of the tophat panel or the top side of the door panel, otherwise the meaning of this mod is negated).
- Install the front tophat panel use parts with `Panel` at start of the file name in this mod.
- Replace the original door latches with the lengthened version in this mod, be careful of the magnet polarity (which should be the same with the original latches).
- Replace the 4 printed parts on the corner of the door with the corresponding parts in this mod. The door handle isn't changed, and it doesn't need removal if the magnet polarity of the latch is kept properly.
